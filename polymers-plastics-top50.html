Castor Oil Derivative Feedstock Chemistry in Polymers & Plastics

Sebacic Acid CAS 111-20-6

Decanedioic Acid (HOOC-(CH2)8-COOH): 10-carbon linear aliphatic dicarboxylic acid produced via alkaline cleavage of castor oil.

  • Bio-Polyamides (Nylon 6,10, 10,10, 4,10): Co-monomer providing high tensile strength, extreme chemical resistance, and 60–100% bio-based carbon.
  • Bio-TPU & Polyols: Esterified with diols into polyester polyols for elastomeric thermoplastic polyurethanes.
  • Low-Temp Plasticizers: Di-octyl sebacate (DOS) & Di-butyl sebacate (DBS) for aerospace, PVC, and synthetic rubber down to -50°C.
  • Light Stabilizers (HALS): Backbone of Tinuvin® 770 and Sabostab® UV 70 UV protective additives.

12-Hydroxystearic Acid (12-HSA) CAS 106-14-9

C18H36O3: Hydroxy fatty acid derived from fully hydrogenated castor oil (HCO). Dual functionality: hydroxyl & carboxyl groups.

  • Internal / External Polymer Lubricants: Reduces melt viscosity, prevents mold adherence, and optimizes cycle times.
  • Color Masterbatch Dispersants: Disperses organic/inorganic pigments (carbon black, TiO2) preventing agglomeration.
  • Polymer Metal Soaps: Calcium, zinc, and lithium 12-hydroxystearates used as non-toxic PVC thermal stabilizers and mold release.
  • Hot Melt Polyurethanes: Monomer in specialty reactive bio-polyols and polyurethane prepolymers.

2-Octanol (Capryl Alcohol) CAS 123-96-6

Secondary Octyl Alcohol (CH3-(CH2)5-CH(OH)-CH3): High-purity co-product of sebacic acid pyrolysis from ricinoleic acid.

  • Specialty Plasticizers: Esterified to Di-sec-octyl phthalate (DIOP/DSOP), dioctyl adipate, and specialty sebacates.
  • Polymer Emulsion Defoamers: High-efficiency defoaming during acrylic/vinyl emulsion polymerization.
  • Chain Transfer & Solvent Carrier: Molecular weight regulator in synthetic resin synthesis and PMMA.
  • Interfacial Tension Depressant: Wetting agent for polymer filler compounding.
