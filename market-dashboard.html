Product Applications & Chemistries

Detailed breakdown of castor derivatives, their industry applications, end products, and chemical roles.

12-Hydroxystearic Acid (12-HSA)

HS 2918.19.00 • Derived from hydrogenation of castor oil.

Industry End Products Role of 12-HSA
Lubricants & Automotive Lithium-complex greases, multi-purpose industrial greases, aviation lubricants Primary thickener. Provides high-temp resistance, water resistance, and mechanical stability.
Cosmetics & Personal Care Deodorant sticks, lipsticks, skin creams, lotions, pomades Gelling agent, emollient, and viscosity modifier. Provides rigid structure for sticks.
Plastics & Rubber PVC compounding, synthetic rubber, plastic wrapping Internal lubricant, plasticizer, and activator. Prevents sticking to molds.
Paints, Inks & Coatings Printing inks, wood finishes, polishes, liquid paints Rheology modifier. Improves flow, prevents pigment settling, enhances gloss.

Sebacic Acid

HS 2917.13.00 • Co-product from high-temperature alkaline cleavage.

Industry End Products Role of Sebacic Acid
Polymers & Plastics Nylon 6,10, bio-plastics, polyurethanes (toothbrush bristles, auto parts) Primary monomer. Gives mechanical strength, chemical resistance, low moisture absorption.
Aviation & Automotive Synthetic ester lubricants (DOS, DBS), engine coolants, antifreeze Base for extreme-temp synthetic lubricants. Highly effective corrosion inhibitor.
Cosmetics Foundations, concealers, sunscreens, hair care Masking agent, film-former, and skin-conditioning agent to improve texture.
Chemical Mfg (Industrial) Metalworking fluids, adhesives, sealants, powder coatings Provides structural flexibility to adhesives; anti-corrosion additive in cutting fluids.

2-Octanol (Capryl Alcohol)

HS 2917.13.00 • Co-product of Sebacic Acid production.

Industry End Products Role of 2-Octanol
Chemical Mfg Plasticizers (Dicapryl Phthalate), surfactants, heavy-duty degreasers Chemical intermediate and powerful, low-volatility solvent.
Paints & Coatings Emulsion paints, baking varnishes, lacquers Excellent solvent and flow-control agent (prevents blistering).
Agrochemicals Pesticides, herbicides, industrial wastewater treatment Highly efficient anti-foaming (defoaming) agent.
Flavors & Fragrances Perfumes, soaps, artificial flavorings Base ester ingredient to create fruity/floral scent profiles.
