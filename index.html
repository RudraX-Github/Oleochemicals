How This Reaction Works (Plain English):

Castor oil consists of an 18-carbon molecule with an oxygen group (-OH) at Carbon-12. Heating it with caustic soda to 275°C breaks the bond right next to that oxygen group, splitting the 18-carbon chain into a 10-carbon di-acid (Sebacic Acid) and an 8-carbon alcohol (2-Octanol).

Reagents In: Purified Ricinoleic Acid (C18)
Active Cleavage Zone: C1 (-COOH) • C9=C10 • C11-C12 • C12 (-OH)
Chemical Mechanism:

The cis-9 double bond forces a 120° planar kink in the aliphatic chain. In Step 3, high thermal excitation targets the C11-C12 single bond alpha to the chiral hydroxyl, snapping the molecule cleanly into two industrial building blocks.
