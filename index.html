Alkaline Pyrolysis of Ricinoleic Acid

Balanced Stoichiometry:
C18H34O3 (Ricinoleic Acid) • Initial Conformation
How This Reaction Works (Plain English):

Castor oil consists of an 18-carbon molecule with an oxygen group (-OH) at Carbon-12. Heating it with caustic soda to 275°C breaks the bond right next to that oxygen group, splitting the 18-carbon chain into a 10-carbon di-acid (Sebacic Acid) and an 8-carbon alcohol (2-Octanol).

Single-Molecule Reaction Action Pi-Complexation

1. Molecular Docking: The electron-rich C9=C10 cis-double bond lands directly on two adjacent metallic Nickel atoms (Ni-Ni surface distance ~2.49 Å).
2. Bond Activation: Nickel's vacant d-orbitals pull π-electrons from the C=C bond, stretching it and dropping its bond order from 2.0 to ~1.4 (preparing it for H2).
3. Nitrogen Blanket Protection: Surrounding N2 molecules act as an impenetrable shield, keeping atmospheric oxygen (<5 ppm O2) from oxidizing the active Ni° metal into dead NiO.

Reagents In: Purified Ricinoleic Acid (C18)
Active Cleavage Zone: C1 (-COOH) • C9=C10 • C11-C12 • C12 (-OH)
Chemical Mechanism:

The cis-9 double bond forces a 120° planar kink in the aliphatic chain. In Step 3, high thermal excitation targets the C11-C12 single bond alpha to the chiral hydroxyl, snapping the molecule cleanly into two industrial building blocks.
